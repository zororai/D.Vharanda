01

Borehole drilling, flushing & installation

New boreholes drilled, old ones flushed back to full flow. We then fit the pump, either AC on the grid or DC on solar, and put up the tank stand and piping.

  • Borehole drilling
  • Borehole flushing
  • AC pump systems
  • DC / solar pump systems
  • Tank stands & tanks
  • Piping & fittings
02

Household solar, supplied & installed

Complete home systems sized to what you actually run: lights, fridge, TV, freezer. We supply the equipment, mount the panels and wire the inverter and batteries.

  • Solar panels
  • Inverters
  • Lithium batteries

  • Roof & ground mounting
  • Solar freezers
